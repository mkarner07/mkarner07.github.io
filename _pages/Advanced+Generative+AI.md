**[Lab Day: Fault Report Triage – from Workflow to Dashboard](/Advanced+Generative+AI+Lab+Day/)**
The case we build in the lab has two parts. First, we turn free-text fault reports into structured tickets with an LLM and a small workflow. You choose one of two stacks: n8n + Gemini or Power Automate + Copilot. Then we build a dashboard on top of those tickets with AI-assisted coding, from a one-prompt prototype to a small app you can publish. The dashboard part works with any AI assistant and the sample ticket data, so you can do it even if your workflow isn't finished. The page has the case story, the build guide with its extension levels, and the prerequisites.

**Please do this before the lab day**: pick your workflow stack and run the five-minute pre-check in the guide. If the AI action in Power Automate is not available in your account, switch to n8n. For the dashboard part, you need at least one AI assistant you can use (Copilot, Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude). A GitHub account is optional, in case you want to publish your result. Use synthetic data only, no real company data. This matters most for anything you publish.
